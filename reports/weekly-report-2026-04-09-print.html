Post-processing

Garment-only sharpening + lighting transfer. Face, skin, and background are pixel-identical to original.

final = original × (1 - mask) + enhanced × mask
06 / 07

Research Plan

Paper contributions

C1: Vietnamese anthropometric model

Calibrated formulas for 5 body clusters. US overestimates chest by ~5.8cm.

C2: Importance-weighted adaptation

Reweight RTR (192K rows) to Vietnamese distribution. ESS: 12.6%.

C3: Fit-aware VTON rendering

Deform garment mask based on predicted fit (tight/normal/loose).

C4: Photo-based body estimation

Estimate height/weight from photo via MediaPipe landmarks.

C5: Fit-accuracy metrics

Size Accuracy, Fit Visual Consistency, Population Fairness.

Paper positioning

FASHN VTON is the backbone (cited). Our contribution: making VTON population-aware and fit-aware.

Target venues

Tier 1: CVPR, ECCV, ICCV. Tier 2: WACV, ACCV, ACM MM. Applied: Fashion & AI workshops.
