05 / 08

Try-On Result Logic

Garment-only postprocessing

Method

FASHN VTON is maskless at inference time. The garment mask is detected afterward and used only for enhancement and compositing.

final = original × (1 - mask) + enhanced_vton × mask
Effect
  • Face, skin, and background remain unchanged.
  • Sharpening is applied only to the garment region.
