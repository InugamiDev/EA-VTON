05 / 06

Result

Garment-only postprocessing

Method

FASHN VTON is maskless at inference time. The garment mask is detected afterward and used for enhancement and compositing.

final = original × (1 - mask) + enhanced_vton × mask
Effect
  • Face, skin, and background remain unchanged.
  • Sharpening is limited to the garment region.
  • The result is visually more stable.
