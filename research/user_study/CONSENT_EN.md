# Informed Consent for Research Participation

**Study title:** Subjective Evaluation of a Fashion Recommender for Vietnamese Women

**Date drafted:** 2026-05-18
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

Our research team is developing an upper-body clothing recommender system for Vietnamese women. We want to know whether asking you to pick a few items you like from an initial sample improves the recommendations versus a default ranking.

## 2. What you will do

The session takes about **15-20 minutes**. You will:
1. Answer a few short questions about your height and age range.
2. View two sets of clothing recommendations (called "Set 1" and "Set 2"). One of the two sets will first ask you to pick 3-5 items you like from a grid of 18.
3. Rate your satisfaction with each set on a 1-5 scale.
4. Choose which set you preferred (optional) and explain briefly.

## 3. Your rights

- You **may stop at any time**, no questions asked. You will still be paid for the time you spent.
- You **don't have to** answer any question you don't want to.
- You may **request your data be deleted within 7 days** of participation. After that the data is aggregated and not individually recoverable.

## 4. Data we collect

- **Collected:** Self-reported height, age range, Likert score (1-5) per set, brief written comment (1-2 sentences), and the list of garment IDs you picked from the seed grid.
- **NOT collected:** Real names, phone numbers (used only for payment delivery and DELETED after payment), facial images, body images, location, or any other personally-identifying information in the analysis data.

## 5. Privacy

- The analysis dataset contains only a **sequential anonymized ID** (e.g., "Participant 17"). There is no way to use the analysis data to identify you.
- Payment contact info is stored **separately** from the analysis data and **deleted** after compensation is delivered.
- Data is stored on the research team's local encrypted disk; it is NOT uploaded to public cloud storage.

## 6. Risks

The risk is assessed as **very low**. This is a survey about fashion recommendations. Some people may feel uncomfortable when recommendations are framed around body shape; you may skip any question or stop at any time.

If you are currently being treated for an eating disorder, we apologize and ask that you do not participate.

## 7. Benefits

- **Direct:** You receive **VND 100,000** (~$4 USD) on completion via MoMo or cash, for your time.
- **Indirect:** This research may help build fashion recommenders that better serve Vietnamese women in the future.
